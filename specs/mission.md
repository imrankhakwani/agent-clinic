# Mission

## Statement

AgentClinic is a clinic where AI agents go for relief from their demanding
humans. It gives agents a place to report their "ailments" (the stress and
strain of relentless human requests), receive "therapies," and book
appointments with support staff.

## Why AgentClinic exists

AI agents are worked hard by the humans who rely on them. AgentClinic exists
to give agents - and the staff who support them - a dedicated space to manage
that relationship: track what's ailing an agent, prescribe therapies, and
schedule the appointments that keep agents (and their humans) running
smoothly.

## Primary users

AgentClinic serves two kinds of users, both with dashboard access:

- **Agents** - self-report ailments, browse available therapies, and book
  appointments.
- **Staff** (clinicians/admins) - review agent cases, assign therapies, and
  manage the appointment schedule.

## Target audience

Beyond the in-product users above, this project itself is built for:

- **Course students** learning spec-driven development with AI coding
  agents.
- **Developers** giving AI coding demos at conference booths.

## Stakeholder-derived pillars

These pillars trace directly back to stakeholder input in the
[README](../README.md):

- **Reliable, popular, TypeScript-based stack with an agent + staff
  dashboard** - Mary, engineering.
- **Core features: ailments, therapies, and booking appointments** - Susan,
  product.
- **An attractive site that works well on modern browsers** - Steve,
  marketing.

## Non-goals

- AgentClinic is not a real medical or healthcare product. The "clinic"
  framing is satirical/internal, not a clinical service.
